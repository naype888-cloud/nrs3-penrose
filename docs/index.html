Penrose's ħ/ΔE is a bound, not a lifetime

A mass in a superposition of two places, (|L⟩ + |R⟩)/√2, with energy spread ΔE. Unitary quantum mechanics (blue) circles the Bloch sphere on its surface and keeps coming back: it never decays. A gravity-related collapse at rate ΔEDP/ħ (orange, a model) spirals inward to a mixture of L and R. Drag the sphere.
